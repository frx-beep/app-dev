# My favorite Series
![]https://github.com/frx-beep/app-dev/blob/readme-edits/OGC.gif)

The series Loki explores and explains how the multiverse works. It follows Loki's journey through the apprehension of the TVA or Time Variance Authority because he created a new branch in reality by escaping the Avengers as seen in Avengers: Endgame. In summary he learns what his true purpose really his while in custody of the TVA. in the end he said one of the best quotes in fiction  “I know what I want. I know what kind of god I need to be… for you. For all of us.” – Loki, expressing selfless growth and responsibility in the finale.
